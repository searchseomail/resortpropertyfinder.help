Common maintenance and structural issues in older Bansko apartments?

zenith_seeker OP
25.01.2025 01:36
I am currently looking at purchasing a studio in one of the older developments in Bansko built during the mid-2000s boom. I have heard mixed reviews about insulation quality, heating efficiency, and potential dampness issues during the winter months. Could anyone who owns or lives in an older building share their experiences regarding common problems to watch out for before committing? Specifically, I am interested in knowing about roof leaks, common area maintenance costs, and general building upkeep.

Check the roof condition immediately. Many mid-2000s buildings in Bansko were built in a rush. If you are buying a top-floor unit, ensure you ask for records of roof repairs, as water ingress is a classic headache here.

Dampness is common if the apartment isn't vented properly. I bought a dehumidifier for my unit, and it changed everything. The winters are harsh, so don't expect these buildings to hold heat like a modern passive house.
