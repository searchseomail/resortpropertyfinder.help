I am a non-EU citizen looking to invest in a vacation home along the Black Sea coast and potentially relocate in the future. I've heard there are specific legal restrictions regarding land ownership versus apartment ownership for foreigners in Bulgaria. Could someone provide a clear, step-by-step guide on the purchase process, necessary legal checks, and how to handle the notary transfer? Any advice on finding a reliable local lawyer to assist with the title deed verification would be greatly appreciated.

As a non-EU citizen, the most important thing to know is that you can freely own an apartment in your personal name, but you cannot own land. If you buy a house with a yard, you must register a Bulgarian company to hold the title to the land. I highly recommend hiring an independent lawyer who doesn't work for the agency selling the property.

The process is fairly straightforward: find the property, sign a preliminary contract, pay a deposit, and then finalize everything at a notary. However, do not skip the 'burden certificate' check. It proves the property isn't mortgaged or under litigation. My lawyer found a hidden debt on a Varna apartment that saved me thousands.

You should look for a lawyer through the official Bulgarian Bar Association website. Avoid 'recommended' lawyers provided by real estate agents, as they often prioritize the commission over your legal protection.
