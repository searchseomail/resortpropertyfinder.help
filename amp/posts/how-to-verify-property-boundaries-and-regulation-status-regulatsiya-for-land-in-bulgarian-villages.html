How to verify property boundaries and regulation status (Regulatsiya) for land in Bulgarian villages?

urban_scribe OP
13.01.2025 10:03
I am currently in the process of purchasing a plot of land in a small Bulgarian village and I am struggling to understand the official status of the boundaries. Could someone guide me on how to verify if the land is currently 'in regulation' (v regulatsiya) and where I can obtain an official cadastral map? I want to ensure that the fences match the legal plot borders before I commit to the sale. Any advice on which local authorities to contact or if there is an online database I can check would be greatly appreciated.

First, check the official Cadastral Agency portal at cadastre.bg. You can search by the plot identifier to see if it is in regulation. Just be aware that fences in villages rarely match the digital map, so don't be alarmed if they are slightly off.

You should definitely hire a licensed surveyor (geodesist). They can perform a 'tracing' of the boundaries to see if the physical fences align with the legal coordinates. It's the only way to be 100% sure before signing the notary deed.

13.04.2025 10:49
To check the status, go to the local municipality (Obshtina) office. Ask for a 'skitsa' (sketch) of the plot. If it says 'ureguliran pozemlen imot' (UPI), it is in regulation. If it just says 'pozemlen imot', it is agricultural land.

The online Cadastral map is great for a general overview, but for a legal transaction, you need the official paper sketch (skitsa) issued by the Agency for Geodesy, Cartography and Cadastre. It costs a small fee and is valid for a limited time.
