arctic_fox_99 OP
11.02.2025 03:01
I am currently looking at purchasing a holiday apartment in Sunny Beach, but I am shocked by the wide range of annual maintenance fees quoted by different complexes. Some developments charge as little as 6 Euro per square meter, while others are asking for 15 Euro or more for what seems like similar amenities. Could long-term owners please share what they consider a reasonable rate for a complex with a pool and basic security? I want to understand what exactly justifies the higher price points before I commit to a property.

In Sunny Beach, anything between 8 and 12 Euro per square meter is generally considered the 'sweet spot' for a standard complex with a pool and security.
