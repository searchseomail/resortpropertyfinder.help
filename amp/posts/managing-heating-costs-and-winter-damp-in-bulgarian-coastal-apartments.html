cipher_soul OP

I own a holiday apartment on the Bulgarian coast and am struggling to keep it dry during the damp winter months without breaking the bank on electricity. The sea air causes significant condensation on the windows, leading to mold issues when the unit is left vacant for weeks at a time. What are the most cost-effective heating solutions or dehumidification strategies for these types of properties? I would appreciate advice from other owners who deal with the high humidity levels in this region.
