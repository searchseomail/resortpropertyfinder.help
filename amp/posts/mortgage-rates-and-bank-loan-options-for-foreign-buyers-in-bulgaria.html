Postbank and DSK are usually the most 'foreigner-friendly', but don't expect miracles. They will require proof of income translated and legalized. Also, keep in mind that apart from the purchase price, you need to budget for maintenance fees, which can be quite high in some Bansko complexes.

Interest rates for mortgages in Bulgaria are currently hovering around 2.5% to 4%, but these are typically for locals. As a foreigner, you might be looking at higher premiums if they approve you at all. I'd suggest contacting a local mortgage broker who specializes in expat clients.
