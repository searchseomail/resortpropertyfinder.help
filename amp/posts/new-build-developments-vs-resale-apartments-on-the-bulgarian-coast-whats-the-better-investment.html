New build developments vs resale apartments on the Bulgarian coast: What’s the better investment?

zenith_seeker OP
19.02.2025 02:21
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday property along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast and I am torn between buying a brand-new development or a resale apartment. While new builds offer modern amenities and better energy efficiency, I am concerned about the potential for construction delays and the lack of established neighborhood character. On the other hand, resale units seem more affordable and are located in prime, mature areas, but I worry about hidden maintenance issues and outdated infrastructure. Could anyone share their experiences or advice on which option holds better long-term value and rental potential?
