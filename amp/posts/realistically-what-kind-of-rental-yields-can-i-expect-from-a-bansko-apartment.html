I am considering investing in a studio or one-bedroom apartment in Bansko for short-term holiday rentals. Are there enough tourists visiting for mountain biking or hiking to make a year-round investment viable, or should I calculate my ROI based solely on the 3-4 months of snow? Management fees in Bansko are high because many complexes are huge and inefficient. If you self-manage, your yield looks much better, but you have to be physically present or have a reliable local cleaner. Expect very low occupancy from April to June and September to November.

Bansko is a winter destination, period. Summer tourism helps pay the maintenance fees, but it rarely generates a significant profit. Calculate your ROI based on a 4-month peak season and consider anything else as 'extra' to avoid disappointment.
