Also, make sure to read up on legal requirements for non-EU citizens if that applies to your situation, as it can complicate the land ownership process.

I've bought two properties in the Varna region. My advice is to find a lawyer who is willing to explain the 'Preliminary Contract' in plain English. This document is legally binding, so you need to be absolutely sure about the terms before you sign anything.
