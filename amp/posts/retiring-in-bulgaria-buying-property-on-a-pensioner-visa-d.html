pixel_wanderer OP
07.01.2025 14:43
I am currently planning my retirement and am seriously considering moving to Bulgaria under the D-type visa for pensioners. I have heard conflicting information regarding whether I need to purchase property before applying for the visa or if a long-term rental contract is sufficient for the residency application. Could anyone share their recent experiences with the property requirement and the overall bureaucratic process for securing legal residency? Any advice on which regions are most expat-friendly for retirees would also be greatly appreciated.
