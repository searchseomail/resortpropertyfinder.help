I recently purchased a holiday apartment in Bulgaria and I am trying to figure out how the municipal tax and garbage collection fees work. I've heard that these are paid annually, but I am unsure about the deadlines, how to calculate the exact amount, and whether I need to register the property with the local municipality myself. Could anyone clarify the process for foreign property owners and if there are penalties for late payments?

Congratulations on your purchase! Yes, you are legally required to register your property at the local municipality within 2 months of signing the deed. If you missed this, do it as soon as possible to avoid fines. You can find more info on the process here: Navigating the Bulgarian Notary Deed process.

The taxes are calculated based on the tax assessment value of the property, not the purchase price. The rates vary by municipality, so check with your local tax office. You usually get a discount if you pay the full annual amount by the end of April.
