The main reason is scale and infrastructure. Bansko saw a massive construction boom in the early 2000s that far outpaced demand, leading to a flooded market. While the prices are low, you really need to look into understanding property taxes and garbage fees in Bulgaria before buying, as maintenance costs can vary wildly depending on the complex management.

It's not just a 'catch'—it's a different market entirely. Labor and materials in Bulgaria are much cheaper than in Switzerland or France, which drives down construction costs. However, don't expect the same resale liquidity you'd find in the Alps; it's a niche market for budget-conscious skiers.
