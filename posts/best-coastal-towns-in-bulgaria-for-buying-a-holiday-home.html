Best coastal towns in Bulgaria for buying a holiday home

orbit_drift
Topic Starter
Posted: 25.01.2025 08:12
I am currently looking into investing in a holiday property along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast and would love some local advice. I am torn between the vibrant atmosphere of Sunny Beach, the historic charm of Sozopol, and the quieter, family-friendly vibe of Lozenets. Could anyone share their experiences regarding rental potential, maintenance costs, and general infrastructure in these areas? I’m looking for a place that offers both a relaxing getaway for my family and a decent return on investment if I decide to rent it out during the peak season.

I own a place in Sozopol and love it. The Old Town has such character, and it attracts a more affluent, family-oriented crowd compared to the party scene in Sunny Beach. Maintenance is manageable, but older buildings need regular check-ups.

Lozenets is beautiful but very seasonal. If you don't mind it being a ghost town in the winter, it's perfect for a relaxing family getaway. Just keep in mind that infrastructure development is slower there compared to the bigger resorts.

Sunny Beach offers the highest rental potential because the season is long and the volume of tourists is massive. However, the maintenance fees in those large complexes can really eat into your profits.

Sozopol is the sweet spot. It stays busy enough to have great restaurants and services, but it’s not as chaotic as Sunny Beach. Property prices there have been holding steady, which is a good sign for long-term investment.
