azure_drift
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 07:21
I own a holiday apartment in Sunny Beach but I struggle to keep up with the electricity and water bills while back in the UK. Does anyone know if there is a reliable online portal or app that accepts foreign credit cards for Bulgarian utility providers? I would prefer not to rely on local property managers for this every month if I can handle it myself. Any advice or experiences with specific payment platforms would be greatly appreciated.
