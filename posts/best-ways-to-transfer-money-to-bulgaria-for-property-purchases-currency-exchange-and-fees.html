Best ways to transfer money to Bulgaria for property purchases: Currency exchange and fees

urban_nomad
Topic Starter
Posted: 02.01.2025 12:17
I am currently in the process of buying a vacation property in Bulgaria and need to transfer a significant amount of funds from my home bank account. I am concerned about the high exchange rate markups and international transfer fees my local bank is charging for the BGN conversion. Could anyone recommend a reliable platform or strategy to minimize these costs while ensuring the transaction is secure for a real estate purchase? I would appreciate hearing about your personal experiences with specific providers or tips on navigating the banking regulations.
