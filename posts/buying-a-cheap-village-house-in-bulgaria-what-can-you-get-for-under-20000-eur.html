Buying a cheap village house in Bulgaria: What can you get for under 20,000 EUR?

prism_vision
Topic Starter
Posted: 27.01.2025 10:37
I am looking to relocate to Bulgaria and have a strict budget of 20,000 EUR for a property. I have seen several listings online for houses in rural villages at this price point, but I am concerned about the hidden costs and the actual condition of these homes. Is it possible to find a habitable house for this amount, or will I be looking at a total renovation project? I would appreciate any advice on which regions are best for affordable yet accessible properties.

Replies (10)

cloud_mapper
Replied: 10.02.2025 16:31
Finding a habitable house for under 20k is possible in regions like Vratsa or Pleven, but be prepared for 'Bulgarian rustic'—meaning outdated wiring and plumbing. Always budget an extra 5-10k for immediate repairs.
mossy_path
Replied: 03.03.2025 07:59
I bought a village house near Veliko Tarnovo last year. At this price point, you are almost certainly looking at a renovation project. Before you commit, make sure you understand the process for opening a Bulgarian bank account, as you will need one for utilities and taxes.
prism_vision
Replied: 15.03.2025 11:48
Stay away from houses that have been empty for more than three years. The roof integrity is usually the first thing to go, and fixing that will blow your entire budget immediately.
stellar_byte
Replied: 07.05.2025 04:40
Look into the Stara Zagora region. It is very affordable, but honestly, don't expect 'move-in ready' for 20k. You’ll be doing a lot of DIY work yourself.
silver_lining_8
Replied: 14.05.2025 03:49
If you want something that doesn't need a total gut job, you might need to look further away from the main cities. The deeper into the village you go, the more house you get for your money.
cloud_mapper
Replied: 20.05.2025 02:42
It’s a common trap to think you can live in these cheap houses immediately. Most need new bathrooms and heating systems. If you aren't handy with tools, you might be better off looking at panelki in Sofia if you have a slightly higher budget for a smaller space.
faded_glitch
Replied: 28.08.2025 06:40
I’d suggest renting in a village for a few months before buying. It gives you a feel for the local community and lets you inspect houses in person rather than relying on misleading online photos.
coffee_and_code
Replied: 01.10.2025 15:13
Don't forget to factor in notary fees, local taxes, and the cost of a translator. Those hidden costs can easily add another 2,000 EUR to your 20k limit.
terra_nova_x
Replied: 04.10.2025 14:26
The North-West is the cheapest part of the country. You can definitely find a solid structure for 20k, but you will almost certainly be replacing windows and internal finishes.
nova_pulse
Replied: 14.12.2025 02:59
Be very careful with 'cheap' properties near the coast. Often they are cheap because they are structurally unsound or have legal issues with the land title. Always hire an independent lawyer.
