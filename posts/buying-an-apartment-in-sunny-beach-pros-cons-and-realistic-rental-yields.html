Buying an apartment in Sunny Beach: Pros, cons, and realistic rental yields

quiet_storm
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 15:59
I am currently considering investing in a holiday apartment in Sunny Beach, Bulgaria, and would love to hear from those who have already taken the plunge. What are the realistic net rental yields I can expect after accounting for management fees and maintenance costs? I am also interested in hearing about the potential downsides or common pitfalls for foreign buyers in this specific market. Is it still a viable investment, or has the area become oversaturated?

I've owned a studio in Sunny Beach for five years. Expect net yields of around 3-5% after all expenses. It's not a goldmine, but it covers costs if you pick a prime location.
cipher_soul
Replied: 20.02.2025 06:27
The market is definitely saturated. You're competing with thousands of other units. Unless you offer something unique, rental income will barely cover your annual maintenance fees.
coffee_and_code

Be careful with management companies. They often take 20-30% of your gross revenue. Make sure you check Understanding Property Taxes and Garbage Fees in Bulgaria before signing anything.
orbit_drift
Replied: 14.04.2025 03:27
It's viable if you treat it as a lifestyle investment rather than a pure cash cow. If you're looking for 10% returns, look elsewhere.

The biggest pitfall is the short season. You only have about 10-12 weeks of high demand. The rest of the year, the resort is basically a ghost town.
