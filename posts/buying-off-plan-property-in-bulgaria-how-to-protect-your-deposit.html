Buying off-plan property in Bulgaria: How to protect your deposit

lost_in_syntax
Topic Starter
Posted: 25.01.2025 00:49
I am currently looking at an off-plan apartment in Sunny Beach and have been asked to pay a 10% reservation fee before the preliminary contract is even drafted. I am worried about the developer's financial stability and want to ensure my money is protected if the project is delayed or cancelled. What specific clauses should I insist on including in the deposit agreement to secure my funds? Are there any escrow services or legal structures in Bulgaria that can safeguard international buyers during this phase?

10% before a contract? That sounds like a huge red flag. Make sure you have a clause that mandates a full refund of the deposit if the preliminary contract isn't signed within 14 days.

In Bulgaria, escrow services aren't as common as in the UK or US, but you can request that the deposit be held by a third-party notary. Don't just wire it to the developer's personal account.

If they refuse to include a penalty clause for delays in the deposit agreement, walk away. You should also check if the developer has a history of unfinished projects in Sunny Beach.

Make sure to get a bank guarantee or a promissory note from the developer. It adds a layer of legal pressure if they fail to deliver on time.
