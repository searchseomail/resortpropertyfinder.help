Can non-EU citizens buy land in Bulgaria? Legal requirements and setting up a company

coffee_and_code
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.01.2025 04:14
I am a non-EU citizen interested in purchasing a plot of agricultural land in Bulgaria for investment purposes. I understand that there are restrictions for individuals, so I am looking into the process of setting up a local company to facilitate the purchase. Could someone clarify the legal requirements for a foreign-owned Bulgarian company to own land and whether there are specific residency or tax implications I should be aware of? Any advice or experiences from others who have gone through this process would be greatly appreciated.

Replies (14)

iron_wisp
Replied: 17.01.2025 06:16
As a non-EU citizen, you definitely cannot buy agricultural land in your personal name. Setting up a Bulgarian OOD (Limited Liability Company) is the standard workaround. Once the company is registered, it is considered a local legal entity and can purchase land freely. Check out this complete guide on buying property in Bulgaria for a breakdown of the process.
silent_echo
Replied: 17.02.2025 02:44
I went through this process last year. The company formation is quick, but you need a local accountant to handle the VAT registration and annual filings. Keep in mind that agricultural land has specific regulations regarding who can farm it, so don't just buy it and leave it empty if you want to avoid potential fines.

The tax side isn't too bad. Bulgaria has a flat 10% corporate tax rate. However, you will have to pay social security contributions if you appoint yourself as the manager of the company, even if you are not drawing a salary.
cloud_mapper
Replied: 20.06.2025 14:59
Be careful with agricultural land. There is a legal requirement that the buyer must have been a resident for a certain period or have specific agricultural qualifications in some cases, though this usually applies more to individuals than companies. Verify this with a local notary.
