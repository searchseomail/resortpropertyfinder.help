Can UK citizens still buy property in Bulgaria post-Brexit?

wild_flower_ink
Topic Starter
Posted: 01.01.2025 15:14
I am a UK citizen currently looking into purchasing a holiday home in the Bulgarian countryside. With the changes following Brexit, I am unsure if there are any new legal restrictions or specific permit requirements for British nationals. Could anyone clarify if the process remains straightforward or if there are additional hurdles I should be aware of before committing to a purchase?

Good news! Brexit hasn't changed the fundamental ability for UK citizens to buy property in Bulgaria. You can still purchase as an individual, but just remember that you should definitely read about navigating the Bulgarian Notary Deed process to ensure everything is legal.
