Can US and Canadian citizens buy property in Bulgaria?

silent_echo
Topic Starter
Posted: 22.03.2025 12:38
I am a Canadian citizen currently looking into real estate investment opportunities in Eastern Europe and have been eyeing Bulgaria. I've heard conflicting information regarding whether non-EU citizens, specifically from North America, are allowed to purchase land or apartments there. Could someone clarify the current legal requirements and if there are any restrictions on owning property as an individual versus through a company? Any advice on the process or potential pitfalls would be greatly appreciated.

Yes, you absolutely can buy property in Bulgaria as a Canadian. Apartments are straightforward, but land is a different story due to EU regulations. You should check out this guide on Can non-EU citizens buy land in Bulgaria? to understand the company structure requirement.

I bought a place in Bansko last year. As an individual, you can own an apartment with land rights, but you cannot own the plot of land itself unless you incorporate. It's a common hurdle for North Americans.
