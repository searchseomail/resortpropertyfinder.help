Choosing between Lozenets, Boyana, and Dragalevtsi for a property investment

hollow_peak
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.01.2025 13:42
I am currently looking to purchase an apartment in Sofia and have narrowed my search down to Lozenets, Boyana, and Dragalevtsi. I am struggling to decide between the central urban vibe of Lozenets and the quieter, nature-oriented atmosphere of the foothills. Could anyone share their experience regarding the long-term investment potential and daily commute from these areas? Any advice on which neighborhood offers the best balance of amenities and quality of life would be greatly appreciated.
