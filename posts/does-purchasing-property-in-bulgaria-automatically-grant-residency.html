Does purchasing property in Bulgaria automatically grant residency?

blaze_horizon
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.03.2025 09:04
I am looking into investment opportunities in Eastern Europe and have been considering buying a holiday home in Bulgaria (currently checking available properties via this link). I have heard conflicting information regarding whether owning real estate gives you an automatic right to residency or a long-term visa. Could someone clarify if there is a minimum investment threshold required for residency and what the process looks like for non-EU citizens? Any advice or personal experiences with this path would be greatly appreciated.

No, buying property in Bulgaria does not automatically grant you residency. It is a common misconception. You still need to go through the standard visa and residence permit application process, which usually requires proving sufficient funds or a specific business purpose.

I went through this process last year. You can own property as a non-EU citizen, but it doesn't give you a free pass for a visa. Make sure you read up on legal requirements for non-EU citizens before putting down any deposits.

It is definitely not automatic. Residency is usually tied to investment programs or employment. If you are looking to purchase, always prioritize checking the legal status of the building. Many people get confused because of the 'Golden Visa' programs in other countries. Bulgaria doesn't have a direct 'property-for-residency' scheme that works like that. You are better off consulting with a local immigration lawyer.

The process for residency is quite strict. Owning a holiday home just gives you the right to stay in your own property for the duration of your visa-free travel allowance (usually 90 days in a 180-day period). Anything beyond that requires a formal residency permit.
