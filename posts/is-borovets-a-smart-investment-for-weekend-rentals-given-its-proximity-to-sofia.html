Is Borovets a smart investment for weekend rentals given its proximity to Sofia?

lunar_orbit
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 12:52
I am considering purchasing a vacation apartment in Borovets primarily to capitalize on the weekend getaway demand from Sofia residents. Given the short drive time and the resort's ongoing development, do you think the rental yields are sustainable year-round or is it strictly seasonal? I would appreciate any insights from current property owners regarding occupancy rates and management challenges in this specific market.

Replies (10)

nova_pulse
Replied: 06.03.2025 03:06
I own a studio near the gondola and honestly, the weekend demand from Sofia is consistent. However, don't expect year-round yields; Borovets is very quiet from May to November. It is great for skiing, but if you want something with more diverse year-round potential, you should check out this comparison on Bansko vs Borovets vs Pamporovo to see which resort fits your investment goals better.
cinder_heart
Replied: 15.05.2025 07:14
The proximity to Sofia is definitely the biggest selling point. My occupancy rate is close to 90% during the ski season, but I struggle to get bookings in the off-season. It is definitely a seasonal play, not a year-round income generator.
velvet_thistle
Replied: 12.08.2025 10:51
Management is the biggest challenge here. Finding a reliable cleaning crew that can turn over your apartment on a Sunday afternoon for a new check-in is surprisingly difficult. You really need a dedicated management company if you aren't local.
pixel_wanderer
Replied: 31.08.2025 12:11
I’ve had a place in Borovets for five years. The weekend crowds are great for cash flow, but the maintenance costs for older complexes can eat into your profits quickly. Make sure to check the annual maintenance fees before you buy.
pixel_wanderer
Replied: 06.09.2025 14:46
Borovets has seen a lot of development, but the infrastructure hasn't kept up. Traffic on the road from Sofia on Friday evenings can be a nightmare, which sometimes discourages last-minute weekend bookings.
iron_wisp
Replied: 30.10.2025 03:48
If you are looking for more than just skiing, you might want to look at the coast instead. While Borovets is good for winter, there are many best coastal towns in Bulgaria for buying a holiday home that offer a much longer rental season compared to a mountain resort.
digital_nomad
Replied: 17.12.2025 04:17
Occupancy is strictly tied to the snow conditions. A bad winter means a bad investment year. I treat my apartment as a lifestyle asset first and a rental second to avoid disappointment.
quiet_storm
Replied: 01.02.2026 08:03
The 'smart' money in Borovets is in high-end, modern apartments with spa facilities. The older, basic units are getting harder to rent out because there is just too much supply on platforms like Airbnb.
copper_fern
Replied: 01.02.2026 16:06
Don't underestimate the utility costs during the winter. Heating a vacant apartment to prevent pipes from freezing is a significant expense that you must factor into your ROI calculations.
nova_pulse
Replied: 04.02.2026 15:46
I’ve considered selling my Borovets place to diversify into the seaside market, as the seasonality there feels less extreme. It is a tough call, but definitely do your homework on the local management fees before committing.
