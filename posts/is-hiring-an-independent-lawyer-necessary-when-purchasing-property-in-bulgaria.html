Is hiring an independent lawyer necessary when purchasing property in Bulgaria?

faded_glitch
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.02.2025 02:57
I am currently looking at a few holiday apartments in Sunny Beach and have heard conflicting advice regarding the legal process. Some agents suggest using their in-house legal team to save money, but I am worried about potential conflicts of interest. Do I really need to hire my own independent lawyer to conduct due diligence and verify the title deeds? I would appreciate any advice from those who have already gone through the buying process in Bulgaria.
