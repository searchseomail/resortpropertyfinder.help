Is it possible to purchase property in Bulgaria using a Power of Attorney while abroad?

mossy_path
Topic Starter
Posted: 30.03.2025 03:03
I am currently living in the UK and have found a property in Bansko that I would like to purchase, but I am unable to travel to Bulgaria for the signing process. Can I legally complete the entire transaction, including the notary deed, by granting a Power of Attorney to a local lawyer? What are the specific requirements for having the document notarized and apostilled in my home country to ensure it is valid under Bulgarian law? I would appreciate any advice from those who have successfully navigated this remote buying process.

Replies (10)

neon_drift
Replied: 01.05.2025 11:08
Yes, it is absolutely possible. I did this last year for a studio in Bansko. You need a specific Power of Attorney drafted in both Bulgarian and English, signed in front of a UK notary, and then apostilled by the UK Foreign Office. Make sure your lawyer is reliable.
mystic_tide
Replied: 03.05.2025 15:35
I've done this twice. The key is to have the POA drafted by your Bulgarian lawyer, then take it to a UK notary public. Once notarized, send it to the FCDO for the apostille. It's a straightforward process if you follow those steps.

It is common practice, but please ensure you have an independent lawyer looking out for your interests. You can read more about why hiring an independent lawyer is necessary to avoid potential pitfalls during the remote transaction.

Make sure the POA is very specific regarding the property details. If it's too broad, some Bulgarian notaries may refuse to accept it. My lawyer in Bansko handled everything remotely without a hitch.

The apostille is the most important part. Without it, the Bulgarian authorities won't recognize your document. Check the UK government website for the latest turnaround times for the apostille service.

I purchased my apartment in Bansko while living in London. I used a local solicitor to draft the POA, signed it at a notary office in London, and sent it via DHL. It worked perfectly for the notary deed signing.
