Is rural house flipping in Bulgaria actually a viable investment strategy?

neon_drift
Topic Starter
Posted: 07.02.2025 00:17
I have been looking at real estate listings in rural Bulgaria and noticed how incredibly cheap the properties are compared to the rest of Europe. I am seriously considering buying a fixer-upper in a village to renovate and resell, but I am worried about the lack of liquidity in those areas. Has anyone here successfully flipped a rural Bulgarian house, or is the market too stagnant to make a profit?

I've looked into this extensively. The short answer is no; rural village houses are 'buy-to-hold' assets at best, not flip assets. The liquidity is almost non-existent outside of major hubs.

I tried this in a village near Veliko Tarnovo. It took me three years to sell the property, and I barely broke even after accounting for the materials and local labor costs.
velvet_sky
Replied: 21.05.2025 06:22
The only way to make money in rural Bulgaria is if you're doing it for yourself to live in. As an investment, you will struggle to find a buyer who isn't looking for a bargain-basement price.
pixel_wanderer

Many people assume that because prices are low, the margin is high. But you have to factor in the lack of infrastructure and the difficulty of finding reliable builders in the middle of nowhere.

Flipping requires a fast turnover. In rural Bulgaria, you are fighting against depopulation trends. It’s the exact opposite of a high-liquidity market.
ember_glow
