Is student rental investment in Sofia still profitable near major universities?

coffee_and_code
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.02.2025 02:58
I am considering purchasing a studio apartment near Sofia University or the Technical University with the goal of renting it out to students. Given the rising property prices in the city, I am curious if the rental yields still justify the initial investment. Are there specific neighborhoods that offer the best balance between high demand and reasonable entry prices? I would appreciate any insights from experienced landlords regarding typical vacancy rates and expected ROI in these areas.

I've been renting out a studio near the Technical University for five years. Yields have compressed due to high purchase prices, but the demand is constant. Just make sure you account for all the unexpected expenses when buying property in Bulgaria, as they can quickly eat into your annual ROI.

Sofia University is a safer bet for long-term value appreciation, but the entry prices are steep. You might struggle to get a high yield immediately, so focus on the capital gains aspect instead.

Look into Studentski Grad. It's the hub for both universities. Prices are high, but vacancy is virtually zero if the apartment is modern and clean.
