Is the Sofia real estate market in a bubble? Price trends and forecast

ember_glow
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 05:51
I have been monitoring property prices in Sofia for the last two years, and the continuous growth seems detached from local wage levels and interest rate hikes. Many experts are warning about a potential correction, yet demand for new construction remains high. I would like to hear your perspective on whether we are currently in a bubble or if this is just a natural market adjustment. What are your forecasts for the upcoming 12 to 24 months?
