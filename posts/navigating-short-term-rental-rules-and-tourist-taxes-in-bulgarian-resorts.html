Navigating short-term rental rules and tourist taxes in Bulgarian resorts

atlas_bound
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.01.2025 09:34
I am planning to list my apartment in Sunny Beach on platforms like Airbnb this summer and I am confused about the current legal requirements. Could someone clarify the registration process for short-term rentals and the specific tourist tax rates applicable in coastal municipalities? I want to ensure I am fully compliant to avoid any potential fines during the peak season. Any advice from experienced hosts on how to handle the reporting would be greatly appreciated.

Don't forget that tourist taxes vary by municipality. Sunny Beach falls under the Nesebar municipality; you need to report guests through the Unified Tourist Information System (ESTI). If you are new to the area, you might find it helpful to read about top coastal towns for vacation properties to see how they compare in terms of regulations.
