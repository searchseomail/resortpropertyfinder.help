Real estate agent fees and commissions in Bulgaria: Who pays?

kinetic_pulse
Topic Starter
Posted: 18.02.2025 02:01
I am currently looking to buy an apartment in Sofia and am confused about how agency commissions work here. Is it standard for the buyer to pay a commission to the agent, or is this fee typically covered by the seller? I have heard conflicting information from different brokers, and I want to understand what the market standard is before I sign any representation agreements. Any insights from recent buyers or property experts would be greatly appreciated.

In Sofia, it is standard practice for the buyer to pay a commission, usually around 2.5% to 3% plus VAT. It is very rare for the seller to cover this unless it is an exclusive listing.
golden_ratio_x
Replied: 09.03.2025 14:03
Be careful before signing anything! Many agents will ask for a commission even if they only showed you the place once. Always negotiate the rate before you start viewing properties.
ember_glow
Replied: 10.03.2025 00:44
I bought an apartment in Lozenets last year and yes, I had to pay 3% to the agency. Make sure you understand all the unexpected expenses when buying property in Bulgaria before you commit to a budget.
iron_wisp
Replied: 22.03.2025 04:22
The market here is 'double-sided'. The agent represents both parties and collects a fee from both. It is a bit of a conflict of interest, so have your own lawyer review the preliminary contract.
tide_runner
Replied: 20.04.2025 15:05
I've seen some agencies try to charge 3% + VAT. Don't be afraid to push back; 2% is often acceptable if the property is expensive.
velvet_sky
Replied: 28.04.2025 04:54
Most brokers in Sofia will push for a buyer's commission. If you find a property listed directly by the owner on sites like Imot.bg, you might avoid this entirely.

It is standard, but you should also check if the agency is actually doing due diligence. Understanding the navigating the Bulgarian Notary Deed (Notarialen Akt) process for property purchase is your responsibility, not just theirs.

There is no 'law' that says the buyer must pay, but it is the industry custom in Sofia. If you don't pay, they simply won't show you the listing.
