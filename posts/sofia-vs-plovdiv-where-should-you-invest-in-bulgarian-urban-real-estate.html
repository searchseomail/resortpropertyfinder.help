Sofia vs Plovdiv: Where should you invest in Bulgarian urban real estate?

prism_vision
Topic Starter
Posted: 02.01.2025 13:23
I am currently looking to diversify my portfolio with a residential property in Bulgaria, but I am torn between the capital, Sofia, and the cultural hub of Plovdiv. While Sofia offers higher liquidity and a massive rental market, I am intrigued by Plovdiv's rapid development and lower entry costs. Which city offers better long-term capital appreciation and stability for a buy-to-let investor? I would love to hear from locals or experienced investors about the current market trends in both cities.

I’ve invested in both. Plovdiv has incredible charm, especially in the Kapana district, but the rental yields are generally lower than in Sofia. Sofia’s market is more mature, meaning less volatility in the long run.
