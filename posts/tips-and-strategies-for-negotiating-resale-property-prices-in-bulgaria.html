Tips and strategies for negotiating resale property prices in Bulgaria

hollow_peak
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.04.2025 05:45
I am currently looking at purchasing a resale apartment in Varna and am wondering how much room there usually is for negotiation with Bulgarian sellers. Is it common to offer significantly below the asking price, or is the market currently too competitive for that? I would appreciate any advice from expats or locals on how to approach the negotiation process professionally without offending the seller.

Replies (11)

mystic_tide
Replied: 09.05.2025 13:55
In Varna, sellers often build a buffer of 5-10% into their asking price. Offering 20% below is usually seen as insulting, but a solid 5-8% reduction is standard if you have cash ready.
blaze_horizon
Replied: 10.07.2025 00:55
Don't rush the process. Always check for proper documentation before making an offer; Understanding Act 16 is crucial to ensure the property is fully legal and avoid future headaches.

Replied: 31.08.2025 14:11
The Varna market is quite active right now. If the apartment is renovated, there isn't much room for negotiation. If it's an older building needing work, you have much more leverage.

I suggest reading up on current trends and future outlook for the Bulgarian real estate market to get a sense of whether prices are cooling off in your specific neighborhood.

Be polite but firm. Use a good local real estate agent; they act as a buffer and can often convince the seller to drop the price more effectively than you can directly.
