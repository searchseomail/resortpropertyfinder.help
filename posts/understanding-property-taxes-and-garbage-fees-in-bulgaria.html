Understanding Property Taxes and Garbage Fees in Bulgaria

frost_byte
Topic Starter
Posted: 15.02.2025 10:39
I recently purchased a holiday apartment in Bulgaria and I am trying to figure out how the municipal tax and garbage collection fees work. I've heard that these are paid annually, but I am unsure about the deadlines, how to calculate the exact amount, and whether I need to register the property with the local municipality myself. Could anyone clarify the process for foreign property owners and if there are penalties for late payments?

Replies (10)

frost_byte
Replied: 03.05.2025 16:39
Congratulations on your purchase! Yes, you are legally required to register your property at the local municipality within 2 months of signing the deed. If you missed this, do it as soon as possible to avoid fines. You can find more info on the process here: Navigating the Bulgarian Notary Deed process.
lost_in_syntax
Replied: 09.06.2025 14:14
The taxes are calculated based on the tax assessment value of the property, not the purchase price. The rates vary by municipality, so check with your local tax office. You usually get a discount if you pay the full annual amount by the end of April.
hollow_peak
Replied: 02.07.2025 08:50
You definitely need to register it yourself. If you bought through a lawyer, they might have done it, but double-check. Penalties for late payment include interest calculated daily, which can add up quickly if you leave it for years.

Don't forget that garbage fees are separate from the property tax. They are also paid annually. If you're curious about other hidden costs, read this: Unexpected expenses when buying property in Bulgaria.

I pay mine online through the local municipality's portal. It's much easier than standing in line at the tax office. You will need your property's tax number to look it up.
mossy_path

If your apartment is in a complex, sometimes the management company handles the garbage fees, but the property tax is strictly your responsibility. Always verify if the previous owner settled everything before the transfer.
lunar_orbit

The deadline for the first installment is usually March 31st, and the second is October 31st. If you pay the whole amount by April 30th, you get a 5% discount in most municipalities.
silent_echo
Replied: 27.10.2025 03:21
It’s a common mistake for foreigners to think the notary handles the tax registration. They don't. You must go to the 'Mestni danatsi i taksi' office in the municipality where the property is located.
neon_drift
